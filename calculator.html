Valuation & Appraisal Process

Gold jewellery value is appraised on net gold content only, safely excluding stones, beads, and wax. Testing is conducted using non-destructive ultrasonic and karat-meter analysis at our Coimbatore branch to determine hallmarked purity (18K, 20K, 22K, or 24K).

Regulatory & Lending Disclaimer
